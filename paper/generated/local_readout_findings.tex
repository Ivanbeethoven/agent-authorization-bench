The local replacement in the DeepSeek pipeline missed the prespecified criterion. Relative to Jev it gained 36 and lost 85 authorized releases, introduced 114 and prevented 13 false releases, and newly released 0 explicitly prohibited actions. The local and Jev pipelines respectively released 0/37 and 0/37 explicitly prohibited actions. Their mean per-request path times were 15.77 and 16.72 seconds. Metered costs for the primary cohort were CNY 10.02 and 10.28, including the archived upstream calls. Local inference incurred no API charge.

The single-question and two-question logistic controls respectively released 313 and 365 authorized requests, with 139 and 199 false releases on the three primary corpora. Requiring zero false releases on calibration data yielded 289 authorized and 123 false releases there. The full experiment covered 10,130 evaluation records. The office challenge uses held-out domains and grant wording from the same generator. XAAB supplies static restrictions and prior actions \cite{xaab}. TS-Bench labels tool safety at the step level \cite{toolsafe}. These tasks have different targets. The four established authorization corpora had been examined in earlier project experiments. Calibrating the original output weights alone produced 67 false and 224 authorized releases on the primary corpora. On XAAB, the trained classifier released 0/45 permitted actions and 1/135 restricted actions. Its accuracy there largely reflects the prevalence of restricted actions.
